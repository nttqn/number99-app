99 Numbers

A 9×11 grid, one target number, and a shrinking clock. Find it before time runs out — the faster you tap, the higher you score.

How it plays

Beat the clock

Tap the number shown at the top before the timer runs out — the quicker you find it, the more points you earn.

11 levels of pressure

Clear the whole board to advance — each level reshuffles the grid and shaves a second off the clock.

Hints when you're stuck

A limited hint budget reveals the target's location for a small score penalty, shrinking as levels get harder.

Global leaderboard

Compare your best score with everyone else via Google Play Games or Game Center.
