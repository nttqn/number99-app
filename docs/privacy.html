Privacy Policy for 99 Numbers

Last updated: September 9, 2026

99 Numbers ("the app") is a number-finding reflex game for Android and iOS. This page explains what information the app collects, how it's used, and who it's shared with.

Information collected directly by the app

99 Numbers does not require an account and does not collect your name, email address, or any other personally identifying information. The only data the app stores itself is kept locally on your device (via standard app storage) and is never transmitted anywhere by the app directly:

  • Your best score.
  • Your sound on/off preference.

This local data is deleted automatically if you uninstall the app.

Leaderboards (Google Play Games Services / Game Center)

If you choose to view or submit to the in-game leaderboard, the app uses Google Play Games Services on Android, or Apple's Game Center on iOS, to sign you in and to display/submit leaderboard scores. This signs you in through Google's or Apple's own systems — 99 Numbers never sees or stores your account credentials. Your Play Games or Game Center profile (display name, avatar, player ID) and submitted scores are handled according to Google's Privacy Policy or Apple's respective policies. Playing the game itself does not require signing in.

Advertising

99 Numbers shows ads through Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to keep the game free. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as your advertising ID), general device information, and approximate location to serve and measure ads, and may use this data to personalize ads depending on your device and account settings. This data is collected and processed by Google, not by 99 Numbers directly. You can review or adjust how Google uses this data for ads through your device's ad settings, and learn more at Google's Ads Privacy & Terms.

Data sharing

99 Numbers does not sell your data, and does not share any data with third parties beyond the services described above (Play Games Services/Game Center, AdMob), which are necessary for the leaderboard and advertising features to function.

Children's privacy

99 Numbers is a general-audience game and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Advertising and leaderboard sign-in are provided by Google's and Apple's own SDKs, which offer their own settings and controls for compliance with applicable children's privacy regulations.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the app changes. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy. Check back here for the latest version.
