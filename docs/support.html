Support for 99 Numbers

Last updated: September 9, 2026

99 Numbers is a number-finding reflex game for Android and iOS: a 9×11 grid of the numbers 1–99, find the target number shown before the timer runs out, and clear the whole board to advance to the next (harder, faster) level.

How to play

  • Tap the cell matching the target number shown at the top before time runs out — the faster you find it, the more points you score.
  • Clear every number on the board to advance to the next level; the board reshuffles and the timer gets a little shorter each level.
  • Use HINT if you're stuck — it reveals the target's location for a small score penalty, and your hint budget shrinks at higher levels.
  • Tap PAUSE (or the device back button on Android) to pause mid-round — the board is hidden while paused so you can't use it to cheat the timer.
  • Clearing the final level is a win; running out of time ends the run.

Common questions

The leaderboard says "not available"

The leaderboard uses Google Play Games Services (Android) or Game Center (iOS). Make sure you're signed in to a Google account (Android) or Game Center (iOS) on your device, and that you have an internet connection.

I don't hear any sound

Check the sound toggle on the pause screen, and make sure your device isn't in silent/ mute mode.

Something's broken, or you have feedback

Please include your device model and OS version (e.g. "Pixel 8, Android 15" or "iPhone 15, iOS 18") along with a description of what happened — it helps a lot in tracking issues down.
